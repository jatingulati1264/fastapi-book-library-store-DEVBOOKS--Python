How can we help you?

Find answers to our most frequently asked questions below.

You can cancel your order for a full refund within exactly 5 minutes of placing it. After 5 minutes, our automated warehouse begins processing the shipment and the order becomes locked to ensure rapid delivery.

Navigate to your "Manage Orders" page and click the "Track Live Location" button on any active order card. This will launch a tracking map and display live status updates from our delivery partners.

For security and logistical purposes, shipping addresses cannot be modified once an order is submitted. Please double-check your address accuracy on the checkout screen before finalizing your purchase.

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express) and PayPal transactions. All payments are securely processed and protected via 256-bit SSL encryption.

Standard shipping typically takes between 3 to 5 business days, depending on your geographic location relative to our main fulfillment centers.

If your books arrive damaged or defective, please contact our support desk within 7 days of delivery. Provide photos of the damaged package and items to initiate an immediate replacement.

Standard shipping is currently free on all orders across eligible domestic regions. Any applicable expedited shipping fees will be clearly indicated on your checkout summary page.

Yes, you can initiate a return within 7 days of receiving your order, provided the items remain in their original, unblemished condition. Contact support to generate a return shipping label.

When you successfully cancel an order within the active 5-minute window, a full refund is immediately triggered back to your original payment method. Bank processing times may vary (typically 1-3 business days).

Guest accounts are designed for standard users to browse books, place orders, and manage their purchase history. Admin accounts have elevated privileges allowing them to add inventory, manage users, and modify site data.
